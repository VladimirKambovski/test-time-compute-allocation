  - HF slug: **CONFIRMED.** `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B` is correct — it's the

    `--default-chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking": false}'` for
    non-thinking mode, `--seed` for determinism, and
    `--logprobs_mode processed_logprobs` for logprob exposure. Free,

## Negative / unexpected results
- Unexpected: despite Qwen3.5-4B being a same-day/near-launch open-weight
  release from a major lab (Alibaba), no mainstream inference aggregator
  currently serves the exact post-trained checkpoint as a simple
  pay-per-token API. Smaller/older Qwen3-4B is far better covered
  (Groq, Novita, OpenRouter, DeepInfra all have it) than the newer
  Qwen3.5-4B, which so far only turns up as a base-model, non-serverless
  listing (Fireworks) or a possibly-stale blog announcement (DeepInfra).
  Worth remembering as a general pattern: brand-new small-model releases
  can lag behind on hosted-API availability even when local weights are
  immediately usable.
